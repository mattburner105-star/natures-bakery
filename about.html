About Nature's Bakery

We believe healthy food should taste amazing. No compromises, no guilt. Just real, fresh ingredients.

Our Story

Nature's Bakery started in 2026 when two friends, Matthew Ghazarian and Tommy Bazikian, got frustrated with the same problem: why does "healthy" always mean "tastes like cardboard"? We loved baked goods, but we didn't love what they did to our bodies.

So we started experimenting: whole wheat flour, oat flour, almond flour, natural sweeteners like honey and maple syrup. After hundreds of recipes (and a lot of taste-testing with friends and family), Nature's Bakery was born.

Today we bake fresh every morning in Glendale, California. Every item on our menu uses real, pronounceable ingredients. No preservatives, no refined sugars, no artificial anything.
